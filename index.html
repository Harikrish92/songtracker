🎙 Voice commands
  • The app starts listening when you open it — just say a command. Later, say "My Logger" first to wake it.
  • Tap the mic anytime to give a command, or tap twice to turn it off.
  • "Add Nilave vaa" — adds to To-do
  • "Add Nilave vaa to completed"
  • "Mark Nilave vaa as sung" or "I sang Nilave vaa"
  • "Remove Nilave vaa" (optionally "from to-do / completed")
  • "Search Nilave" / "Clear search"
  • "Stop listening" — turns the mic off
You can say it all at once: "Hey MyLogger, add Nilave vaa".
